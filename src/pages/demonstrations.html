Demonstrations

Watch imperfect delivery in the vehicle recording, then follow a fact as it is corrected and withdrawn.

Six cars, imperfect reports

The Workbench Scenario Simulator generates position reports for six cars and deliberately disrupts delivery. It delays, duplicates and drops reports, and makes individual cars go silent before they resume reporting.

Each marker shows the latest position ZooGlass has accepted for that car. When an older report arrives after a newer one, the marker holds its newer position. A duplicate leaves it there too.

In this recording, watch Car 3 during its source outage. The other cars keep updating. The silent car’s marker stays at its last known position, and the display indicates that the report is ageing. The viewer also uses this grey, dashed style when its own connection is interrupted. When a fresh report arrives, the marker can move again.

The vehicle display calculates report age from the source’s observation timestamp. This is a demo rule, distinct from ZooGlass’s configurable receipt-age policy, which measures time since an accepted contribution.

Corrections and withdrawals

Correcting a goal and its score

A match feed reports a goal, corrects the scorer and later withdraws the goal. Two views consume that same feed with different revision strategies. Each exposes the retained goals and a total of their points.

Follow the corrections

"goals": {
  "sourceId": "match-feed",
  "type": "goal",
  "mode": "activeFacts",
  "facts": {
    "revision": "latestFactRevision",
    "maxRetainedFacts": 200,
    "minimumBirthSequence": 1
  }
}

Keep the original

Use the same field definition in another view, changing the revision strategy to: 

"revision": "firstFact"

The source does not need to publish a second version of its feed. Each field applies its own strategy.

These are field definitions inside a view configuration. The linked guide supplies the envelope format and operating instructions.

Add a sumActiveFacts field for the same source and event type, using property: "points" and the same fact revision settings. ZooGlass maintains the goal collection and score together. The payloads below credit one point, then correct that contribution to two points.

Expected collection and score after each publication
Source publication latestFactRevision firstFact
Create · birth 40, revision 1
Scorer: Player A, points: 1		 Player A · score 1 Player A · score 1
Revise · birth 40, revision 2
Scorer: Player B, points: 2		 Player B · score 2 Player A · score 1
Retract · birth 40, revision 3 Empty collection · score 0 Player A · score 1
Retry that same retraction Unchanged Unchanged

The correction and retraction have new publication sequences but refer to the same fact birth. The first-fact field rejects those alterations as immutable. The latest-revision field follows them. If the source retries the same retraction within the retention window, ZooGlass acknowledges it as a duplicate with the original version and both views’ recorded outcomes. The collections and scores stay as shown in the table.

What this removes from the application

The application reads the retained collection and calculated score. It does not have to maintain a revision index, remove withdrawn facts, adjust the total, suppress repeated publications or rebuild those decisions after a durable restart.
